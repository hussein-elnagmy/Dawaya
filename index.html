ABOUT DAWAYA
A local guide
to clearer answers.

      
Dawaya brings medicines found in Egypt into one searchable directory. Look up brand names, active ingredients, manufacturers, classifications, routes, and listed prices.
Medicine listing data is loaded from an open GitHub dataset. Educational pharmacology notes are separately curated and are not a substitute for an approved product leaflet.
